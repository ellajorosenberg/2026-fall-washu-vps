Chia Amisola's "There are No Gardens on the Internet"

I really enjoyed playing around with this website. The imagery was very intriquing and beatiful, with the griany texture and the use of different elements creating a unique and immersive experience. I really liked how each item you click on takes you to a different description or even a secondary page. The site is very interactive and engaging. The game-like set-up is a great way to engage the viewer and keep them interested throughout the experience.

I love the incorporation of sound into the wesbite, which adds to the atmosphere created. It's very satisfyng to hear the noises it makes when you click on different buttons, although some of them were a little stress inducing 🫣. I also found some of the interactive elements a tiny bit frustrating when it wouldn't click, or I couldn't find something --- but I suppose that's how the game works.

Overall, I found the website to be a very creative and engaging experience, with a strong emphasis on interactivity and sensory elements. It's a great example of how digital media can be used to create immersive and memorable experiences for users.
